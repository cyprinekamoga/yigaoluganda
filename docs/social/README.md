# Instagram starter pack: Ngaali

Ready-to-post images (1080 × 1350, Instagram's 4:5 feed size) with captions, in the style that works
for Duolingo: the mascot is a character, posts entertain first, and every post teaches one real
phrase. Remake or add posts with `node scripts/make-social.mjs`.

All Luganda here comes from the course (`content/`). Have a native speaker look at anything new
before it goes out, as with the course.

## Who Ngaali is online

Ngaali is the grey crowned crane, the bird on Uganda's flag. She stands on one leg and teaches Luganda.

- **Dramatic and a little judgy.** She notices when the drum goes quiet or someone answers Jjajja in English.
- **Soft about family.** Grandparents, aunties and Sunday calls are why people learn.
- **Never mean about accents or heritage.** She teases the situation, never the person.
- **Always teaches.** Every post leaves the reader with one phrase they can use today.
- **Promises only what's live.** No kids mode or certificates in posts until they are in the app.

Suggested bio:
> Learn Luganda with Ngaali 🇺🇬 5 minutes a day · in English & svenska · Gyebale ko! 👇
> yigaoluganda.com

## Reels (where the reach is)

Reels reach the most people. Film these on a phone, or ask Claude to animate them with Ngaali.

1. **“Ngaali rates your Luganda”**
   - Use a trending sound.
   - On-screen text: *“Me saying ‘Oli otya’ with my London accent.”*
   - Cut to a slow zoom on Ngaali’s side-eye from post 1.
   - End card: *“Ndi bulungi. Practise with Ngaali. Link in bio.”*
2. **“Aunties at full speed”**
   - A real Luganda speaker records a fast voice-over: greetings, family news.
   - On-screen text: *“POV: you understood the first word.”*
   - End card: *“Yogera mpola! (speak slowly)”*, with Ngaali thinking.
3. **“Day 1 vs day 30”**
   - Day 1: someone stumbles over *Gyebale ko*.
   - Day 30: they greet the shopkeeper with it, and the shopkeeper answers *Kale*.
   - Show the drumbeat in the app between the two.
4. **“The Sunday call”**
   - A real family films a child or adult greeting Jjajja in Luganda on a video call. Get written permission first.
   - On-screen text: *“Week 1 of Yiga Oluganda.”*
   - This is the one people share.
5. **A recurring series: “Ngaali’s crown rating”**
   - Followers send a clip of themselves saying the word of the week.
   - Ngaali rates it from 1 to 9 crown feathers. Always kindly, always with a tip.

**Stories, every other day:**
- A quiz sticker, e.g. *“What does Kale mean?”*
- A poll, e.g. *“Did you say Gyebale ko this week?”*
- Reposts of followers who tag you.

**Replying to comments** is part of the persona. Answer in Ngaali's voice: short, a bit cheeky, and include a phrase.

## Before posting

- **Luganda:** have every new line checked by a native speaker.
- **Music:** use Instagram's own sounds. Licensed tracks can get a Reel muted.
- **Real people:** have permission before posting anyone. Never film children without their parents' written consent.
- **Kids mode and certificates:** don't announce them until they are live.
